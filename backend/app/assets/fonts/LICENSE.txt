DejaVu fonts — license notice
=============================

The two TrueType files in this directory (DejaVuSans.ttf,
DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf) are used only to embed a Unicode-capable font in
server-generated PDF exports, so accented and non-Latin historical text is
not mangled.

DejaVu fonts are based on Vera fonts and are released under a free license
(the Bitstream Vera Fonts Copyright plus the DejaVu changes, which are in
the public domain). Full text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

Upstream: https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts
Package used: dejavu-fonts-ttf 2.37.3 (via jsDelivr).

These files are assets, not source code: do not edit them.
